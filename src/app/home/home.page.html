What Is the Order of Thekla?

The Order of Thekla is an ecumenical monastic community rooted in the courage and witness of Saint Thekla. Our nuns make commitments (not vows), choosing a life shaped by prayer, holiness, simplicity, and service. We welcome all women into our spiritual family—nuns, novices, companions, and seekers.

Discover Thekla's Story

Thekla, the disciple of Paul, is one of the earliest Christian heroines— a preacher, healer, and protector of women. Her story has changed lives for nearly two thousand years.
