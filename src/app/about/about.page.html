About the Order of Thekla

About the Order of Thekla

The Order of Thekla is an ecumenical Christian sisterhood dedicated to holiness, discipleship, and the courageous faith of Saint Thekla — the first female disciple of Saint Paul and a woman whose devotion to Christ transformed her life and the lives of those around her. Her story resonates deeply with Christian women who have endured trials, walked through fire, and emerged with a renewed love for God.

A Calling Rooted in an Independent Journey

My journey began as an independent nun, long before the Order existed. I lived a consecrated life out of obedience and love for God, shaped by Scripture, prayer, and a desire to devote myself wholly to Christ. For many years, I believed that being a nun meant belonging to one specific denomination, but the Lord gently led me into a broader, richer understanding of His Church.

Through Christian Leaders Institute, I was introduced to true ecumenicism — unity centered on Christ, rooted in Scripture, and lived out in love, without erasing the beautiful diversity within the Body. That understanding gave language and definition to what my heart had already known: that consecrated life is possible outside of traditional denominational structures.

Encountering Saint Thekla

During my theological studies, I learned the story of Saint Thekla, and her life struck a chord within me. Her courage amid persecution, her unwavering devotion to Jesus, and her boldness to follow God's call regardless of opposition mirrored portions of my own story. I recognized in her a spiritual companion — someone who walked through trials and emerged more deeply aligned with Christ.

This recognition became the seed of the Order of Thekla, a sisterhood shaped by her example and dedicated to women who desire a consecrated life centered on Jesus.

A House Given to God

My home gradually became a place of ministry, prayer, and discipleship. I formally consecrated it to God, and in early 2025 it was officially set apart as One Assembly, a small ecumenical home church dedicated to worship, discipleship, Scripture, sacraments, and Christian fellowship.

The Order of Thekla will operate under the spiritual protection and authority of One Assembly, which serves as the covering for our ministry, our gatherings, and our commitments.

Since 2020, God has sent help in tangible ways. Many charities, ministries, and religious organizations have assisted with repairs and improvements to the home, preparing it to receive guests, hold services, and one day welcome a novice or another nun. Each improvement has felt like confirmation that God is preparing a place for His daughters.

Mother Church: Experience Community Church

While One Assembly is our home base and ecclesial covering, we are also members of Experience Community Church in Murfreesboro, Tennessee. We consider ECC our mother church, the larger spiritual family that nourishes our walk with Jesus.

Because of current transportation limitations with my powerchair, especially on weekends, I participate in ECC through:

  • Online worship and sermons
  • Tithing
  • Life group involvement and discipleship

The Order of Thekla honors and receives spiritual nourishment from Experience Community Church while living out its unique calling within One Assembly.

A Date Set Apart

The official founding of the Order of Thekla is planned for April 17, 2026.

This date is deeply personal: it commemorates the twentieth anniversary of my spiritual rebirth in the spring of 2006. Although my "spiritual birthday" centers around March 26, the entire season marks the time when God drew me out of confusion and into light, healing, and purpose. The founding of the Order is an offering of gratitude for what He did — and a consecration of the years to come.

Our Calling

The Order of Thekla is dedicated to:

  • Christ-centered consecrated life
  • Ecumenical unity grounded in Scripture
  • Holiness, simplicity, and purity of heart
  • Serving women in need, especially the wounded or marginalized
  • Daily discipleship and spiritual formation
  • Teaching, healing, and pastoral care
  • Courageous Christian living in the spirit of Thekla

We do not take formal vows but make commitments of intention — rooted in Jesus' teaching and shaped by humility, love, and devotion.

A Home for God's Daughters

The Order welcomes:

  • Women who feel called to consecrated life
  • Divorced or single women whose children are grown
  • Independently consecrated sisters seeking spiritual family
  • Women longing for a life of prayer, study, service, and holiness

Local or online, each sister brings her story, her gifts, and her devotion to Christ.

What "Ecumenical" Means

As an ecumenical community, the Order of Thekla welcomes women from all Christian traditions. We honor the diversity of Christian faith while maintaining our commitment to core Christian beliefs and practices. Our community is a place where women from different denominational backgrounds can come together in shared devotion to Christ.
