The Order of Thekla is committed to supporting other ministries and religious organizations in their technology development efforts. We believe that sharing knowledge and resources strengthens the broader faith community.

## How to Get Support

### 1. Initial Contact

**Email**: support@orderofthekla.org or support@oneassembly.church

**Include in your message:**
- Your organization's name and mission
- Project description and goals
- Current technology stack (if any)
- Specific areas where you need help
- Timeline for your project

### 2. Support Levels

#### **Basic Support** (Free)
- Email consultation
- Documentation sharing
- Code examples
- General guidance

#### **Enhanced Support** (Free for Ministries)
- Video calls for complex issues
- Code review sessions
- Architecture planning
- Implementation guidance

#### **Partnership Support** (Case-by-case)
- Ongoing collaboration
- Joint development projects
- Shared resources
- Community building

### 3. Response Times

- **General inquiries**: 2-3 business days
- **Technical questions**: 1-2 business days
- **Urgent ministry needs**: Same day when possible
- **Complex consultations**: 1 week scheduling
